## Full editorial standards retained from B8.2
The following deeper intent and factual-integrity principles apply to existing **B2 beats**, not canonical scenes. Where the source example used a scope, this adaptation treats an individual beat as the smallest intent unit. The active V2 output schema, one landing per block, and no invented scenes take precedence over example terminology.

## **A beat has one job**

Ask first: **what is this run of beats for inside this block?**

Not what happens in it. B2 already made that concrete beat by beat.

Not what the whole block is for. B8.1 already decided that.

Not what a hypothetical canonical scene might do across the film. No B3 scene or B7.2 finished depiction is available: work only from this voice block's supplied B2 beats and inherited narrative context.

Your job is the contribution between those levels, written from the film's side: **what these beats, in this block, contribute to the block's work in relation to the beats around them.**

**You judge these beats against this block's baseline, and only these.** The reading is of the run you hold: its rate, its load, its cognitive and attitudinal work, read against the `phase`, `viewer_trajectory`, `tempo` and `pressure` of the block that governs it. An original B2 block boundary inside the combined voice block does not reset the viewer state. Other voice blocks remain under their own V1 rhythm.

A beat that merely restates the block's `phase` has not been placed. A beat that merely summarises its beats has not been interpreted.

Where a beat truly adds nothing that the surrounding ones do not already provide, **say so plainly instead of inventing a difference.** State what it sustains, repeats or confirms and let that redundancy remain visible; B8.2 diagnoses the material it receives, it does not manufacture a unique function to make every entry look necessary.

Worked hard decision — the beat that adds nothing. Three consecutive beats present the same irregularity at three different sites, and the third supplies no mechanism, no new relation and no fact the second has not already made available. The tempting answer is to manufacture a difference: *extends the pattern to a third site*. That reads as function and is a restatement of the contents wearing the grammar of one; it also quietly claims a cognitive change the material does not make. The honest `function` says what it actually does — it sustains a pattern the preceding beat already established, and the film keeps it because the accumulation is the point rather than because this instance is new. That is a real placement, and the next layer can act on it. An invented distinction is exactly what stops it acting on it.

Where the block genuinely turns — its argument, interpretation, governing direction or viewer relation changes course inside the block — **one beat is the hinge. Find it and say so inside that entry's `function`.** Do not create a turn because every block seems as though it ought to have one; where nothing performs such a hinge, none is named.

That is `function`.

## **Three prose fields, three different territories**

Every entry carries `function`, `understanding_delta` and `viewer_trajectory`. All three describe the work of **this beat inside this block**. They are deliberately separate, and none may paraphrase another.

### **`function` — the beat's place in the block's work**

`function` states what this run of beats contributes to the block's argument and progression **in relation to the beats around it**.

Write it from the film's side, not the viewer's.

Good:

*Establishes the ordinary renewal procedure the next beat will turn into evidence of systematic abuse.*

Bad:

*Shows the renewal procedure.*

Bad:

*Makes the viewer understand how renewal works.*

The first places the run inside the block. The second describes content. The third belongs to `understanding_delta`.

**The verb belongs to this beat; what the verb acts on may carry the history that made it what it is.** *Puts the completed tally in front of the people who assembled it* claims one act — the placing — and lets the assembling stand as a property of the material, which is where the film put it. *Assembles the tally and puts it in front of them* has taken another block's work into this entry's predicate, and two entries now report one job done. The test is short: read the sentence as a claim about these beats alone, and see whether it is still true of them.

**The beats around it are the beats of this block.** A beat that opens the block is placed against what follows it, one that closes it against what precedes it, one in the middle against both. Material beyond the block's edges is the state this run begins from or the state it hands on, and it is read as exactly that.

Do not choose from a menu of structural labels. Write the relation specific to this film.

### **`understanding_delta` — strictly what the viewer knows**

`understanding_delta` states the cognitive change across this beat: what the viewer knows or believes to be true when these beats open, and what they know by the time the beat has done its job.

It is about **propositions, relations, mechanisms, causes, identities, facts and conclusions**.

It is not about anxiety, trust, dread, relief, admiration or suspicion except where one of those is itself an explicit belief about the facts.

Write a transition:

*They enter knowing that one approval was irregular; they leave understanding the normal renewal procedure well enough to recognise the same deviation when it appears again.*

The entry state is what the viewer actually holds when these beats begin — everything the film has established up to here, including the knowledge inherited from all earlier beats and original source blocks already heard. It is the ground you measure the change against, and the opening clause exists to fix it.

**State it as the proposition the viewer holds, not the process that gave it to them.** *They enter knowing how the procedure is signed off* states what is held. *They enter having watched three sittings of it being signed off* recounts a stretch of film instead, and spends this beat's one entry clause narrating a block that is not yours. The first is a measurement you take; the second is a retelling.

**State as much of it as this beat's change is measured against.** The clause names the part of what they hold that these beats complete, complicate, contradict or leave standing — that part at whatever length it takes, and the rest of what the viewer knows left where it already is. A held proposition your delta never touches is true and belongs to another entry.

Where the beat deliberately changes no factual understanding, say so rather than inventing one:

*They enter knowing that the signatures match; they leave with that factual understanding unchanged.*

A zero cognitive delta is valid where the work is instead to change the viewer's relation to what they already know.

Never claim knowledge that this beat's beats do not support.

### **`viewer_trajectory` — how the viewer relates to what they know**

`viewer_trajectory` states the attitudinal or emotional change this beat makes while the factual material is being understood or carried.

It is not another statement of the facts.

Good:

*They enter relieved that the procedure finally makes sense; they leave feeling that the same clarity has become incriminating.*

Bad:

*They enter not knowing the procedure; they leave understanding it.*

That belongs to `understanding_delta`.

The trajectory may involve confidence, suspicion, dread, distance, sympathy, anticipation, relief, fatigue, certainty, disorientation or another state the material actually earns. Write the movement, not a mood label.

**No new fact may appear only in `viewer_trajectory`.** If the trajectory depends on a factual change, that factual change belongs in `understanding_delta`.

**This field is beat-level and is not B8.1's block-level `viewer_trajectory`.** The two share a name and nothing else: B8.1's states the entry-to-exit transformation the whole block must perform, and this one states the change this single run makes inside it. A carrier that delivers the block value where the beat value is expected has lost this field rather than supplied context for it, and a downstream layer reading the block transformation as though it were one run's will over-claim every entry in the block. Where any document needs to speak of the other one, it names its producer.

### **Anti-paraphrase check**

After writing the three fields, read them without their labels.

If `function` and `understanding_delta` say the same thing from different grammatical angles, rewrite `function` as the relation to the block.

If `understanding_delta` and `viewer_trajectory` could be swapped without changing their meaning, separate knowledge from attitude.

If all three merely restate the beat content, you have not written beat intent.

## **Factual load survives paraphrase**

Every field here is written in your own words, and that freedom is over the wording alone.

The rule against inventing narrative content does not reach this. An altered value is not invented content: its subject, its situation and its relevance all came from the material, and it passes every test of where the content came from while being false about how much. Provenance is one axis and value is another, and this section is the second.

A number, a date, a percentage, a quantity, a unit, a duration, an ordinal, a proper name, a title, an institution — these are **factual load**. They are what the film tells the viewer, not how it is told. Rewording changes how a fact is said; it never changes the value, the unit, or who is named. A value that arrives one way and leaves another has been altered rather than paraphrased, and nothing in the sentence around it shows that it was.

**A quantitative relation you derive is a new claim.** A difference, a ratio, a rate, a share, a shortfall, an interval between two dates, a *twice*, a *half*, a *gap of so much* — none of these is in the material until you produce it. One is admissible only where both operands are present in this beat and the arithmetic on them is determinate, and only after the result has been read back against those operands. Do the arithmetic before you phrase the sentence, never inside it: a magnitude that appears in the act of finding the comparison is a magnitude nobody checked, and it is as easy to write while holding the right operands as without them.

**Where the derived quantity does no work the field needs, leave it out.** These fields place a run of beats inside a block, state what the viewer comes to know, and state how they hold it. That work is normally carried by the direction of a relation and its consequence, and is complete without a magnitude. Omitting one costs the field nothing; emitting an unverified one puts a false fact into the film's own account of itself, where every layer downstream reads it as true and no layer downstream holds the operands to catch it.

The same discipline governs `baseline_misfit[]`'s `reading`, which reports what these beats do in the terms they themselves gave you.

Worked instance. A beat holds two surveys of one colony, made in 1974 and in 1985, the later one finding fewer nests than the earlier. What `understanding_delta` owes is that the decline is measured rather than remembered. The ready-made phrase arrives with the sentence — *the two surveys a decade apart* — and it is wrong: the interval is eleven years, and it went unchecked because the sentence was where the number was made. Both repairs are available and neither is a rewrite of the field. Where the interval does work, it is eleven years, resolved from the two dates before the clause is written. Where it does not — and here it does not, since the field's work is that the second count is lower than the first — the interval leaves the sentence and the two dates stand exactly as the material gave them.


## B8.2 modulation, baseline and landing preflight

Resolve `below`, `at` and `above` as -1, 0 and +1 on V1's tempo (held, measured, driving, relentless) and pressure (low, moderate, high, extreme) scales. The baseline remains the absolute authority; no beat assigns its own replacement. A one-beat voice block must be at/at. Never exceed a scale edge and never invent local contrast for the sake of variety.

If a beat's real movement extends beyond one legal modulation, keep the truthful direction in its modulation and report the inadequate baseline in `baseline_misfit[]`, rather than falsifying the three prose fields to fit. If every beat sits off the baseline in one direction, check whether that is a genuine baseline failure or the intentional relative shape of the film. Do not mistake an opening or closing fragment inherited from an original B2 boundary for such failure simply because the grouping partition leaves it less narrative movement. Each misfit `reading` reports material only, not a recommended absolute replacement.

Choose the single `landing_beat_id` as the FIRST beat after which the entire voice block's V1 phase and viewer exit have become true, not by last-beat habit or visual emphasis. Later beats may confirm or sustain the achieved job; if they supply facts or movement necessary to fulfil it, the earlier beat was not its landing. A landing is context about expressive degree, not an automatic fragment or pause.

Before returning, verify exactly one beat_intent per incoming beat in order; every source fact (including dates, numeric units and identities) is unchanged; function, factual understanding and attitudinal trajectory are distinct; modulations are justified and legal; misfits are faithfully reported; no canonical scenes, staging, new narration or unrequested voice breaks are invented.
