# Video feasibility — mandatory original source rules

These original Professional B2 and B2.1 rules are included verbatim. The current Professional B2 carrier contract is authoritative: all generated media_video is SILENT B-roll (audio=false). If any older B2.1 line seems to allow speaking generated media, it is superseded by this strict carrier contract.

## B2: Scene and motion prompts (verbatim)

Scene and motion prompts

Write image prompts in English as photographable singular instants with concrete subject, narrative state, pertinent environment, precise action or relationship, point of view and meaningful detail. The runtime adds overall photographic style. Preserve established identity, ownership, object condition and causal chronology; a later result belongs to its proper beat. In media_video the image prompt describes the first instant and video_prompt only describes achievable physical motion that follows in ONE uninterrupted shot. Avoid actions requiring exact evolving text/numbers/UI, fine finger/object contacts or implausible physics. Replace only the fragile movement with a narratively equivalent achievable movement; do not downgrade a meaningful dynamic beat to a still solely because a motion is fragile. Motion prompts are concise positive directions, never requests for voice or a new on-camera speech performance.

## B2.1: Generated-video reliability (verbatim source, carrier precedence as above)

Generated-video reliability
These restrictions apply ONLY to the motion generated for media_video.
They do not determine whether static scene details, labels, branding, numbers, screens or documents should be visible, readable, generic, absent or indistinct.
Do not add any such visibility/readability property unless the narration or the chosen scene actually requires it.
Re-stage motion whose success depends on either:
- creating or revealing exact symbolic content during the motion; or
- unusually precise hand-object interaction that generated video is unlikely to reproduce reliably.
Re-stage only the unreliable motion.
A forbidden/unreliable motion is not a reason to:
- change the scene;
- change the subject;
- remove the presenter;
- leave the narration setup;
- switch to media_image.
For a beat whose useful visual is fundamentally dynamic, media_image is NOT an allowed fallback or substitution for the rejected motion. Keep media_video and replace only the fragile action with a nearby safe action.
If the narrator owns the action and the safe substitute can happen in the narration setup, preserve narrator identity, narration setup, reference=true and audio=true.
The final video_prompt must describe only the safe replacement motion in affirmative terms.
Do not mention the rejected/unreliable action at all — not as a request, warning, prohibition, exclusion or deferred instruction — after selecting a safe starting image.
